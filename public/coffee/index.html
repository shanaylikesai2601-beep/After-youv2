Our Heritage

Born in the Highlands

In the misty peaks of Ethiopia's Yirgacheffe region, our founder discovered a bean unlike any other. A century later, Cielo still sources from that same terraced slope — elevation 2,200 meters, rainfall 1,800mm annually, hand-picked at dawn.

Every bean in every bag traces to a single origin. No blends. No shortcuts. Just the purest expression of terroir, preserved from hill to roastery.

The Process

Where Science Meets Soul

01

Sourcing

Direct trade partnerships with 24 family-owned farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala.

02

Sorting

Every bean hand-sorted twice. Defect rate below 0.3% — five times stricter than specialty grade.

03

Roasting

Small-batch air roasters. Real-time AI-curve profiling. Each roast logged and traceable via batch code.

04

Resting

72-hour degassing in nitrogen-flushed tanks. Beans reach peak flavor on day 4 after roast.

05

Cupping

Every batch blind-cupped by a Q-Grader. Scores below 88 are diverted to wholesale, never retail.

06

Shipping

Oxygen-free, one-way valve packaging. Roast-to-door in under 72 hours, guaranteed.
