Our Philosophy

Slow beans.
Fast friends.

Aurora began as a weekend obsession — a single air-popper, green beans from a farmer in Guatemala, and a notebook full of failed roasts. We believed then what we believe now: that coffee is not a commodity. It is a relationship between soil, producer, roaster, and drinker.

Today we source from three direct-trade partnerships in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra. Every lot is cupped, graded, and roasted to order. We never blend for cost. We blend for harmony.

Direct Trade 100% of our beans
Small Batch 8–15 lb roasts
Carbon Neutral Since day one
