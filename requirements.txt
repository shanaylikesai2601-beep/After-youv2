Coffee Company Landing Page Requirements

Sections:
1. Hero
   - Full-width background image or video
   - Company logo and tagline
   - Call-to-action button (e.g., "View Menu", "Order Now")
   - Optional: animated elements

2. Menu
   - Display of coffee offerings (espresso, latte, cappuccino, etc.)
   - Item name, description, price
   - Filter options (hot, cold, seasonal)
   - Add to cart button (if e-commerce) or "Learn more"
   - Responsive grid layout

3. About
   - Company story and mission
   - Founder/team photos
   - Values (sustainability, quality, community)
   - Timeline or milestones
   - Embedded video or image gallery

4. Contact
   - Contact form (name, email, message)
   - Map showing location(s)
   - Business hours and address
   - Phone number and email icons (click-to-call/email)
   - Social media links

5. Footer
   - Copyright notice
   - Quick links (Home, Menu, About, Contact)
   - Newsletter signup form
   - Social media icons
   - Payment method icons (if applicable)
   - Accessibility statement

Required Features:
- Responsive design (mobile-first)
- Navigation menu (sticky or hamburger on mobile)
- Smooth scrolling for anchor links
- Image optimization (lazy loading)
- Accessible (ARIA labels, keyboard navigation)
- SEO-friendly meta tags
- Fast loading performance
- Cross-browser compatibility
- Interactive elements (hover effects, button animations)
- Form validation (client-side)
- Integration with email service for newsletter/contact
- Optional: Online ordering system (if applicable)
- Optional: Blog or news section
- Optional: Customer testimonials carousel
